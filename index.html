How We Chose the Relocation Sites

Decoupling latency-critical cloud (<2ms, 50–100 km) from latency-tolerant AI training (10–20ms, 150–400 km).

Mathematical Siting Scoring Formula:
• Sustainability Uplift = 0.35×(ΔRE Share) + 0.25×(ΔSolar CF) + 0.25×(ΔWind CF) + 0.15×(ΔWater Stress)
• Distance Penalty = 0.5×(Distance/50 km) for Cloud  |  0.4×(Distance/100 km) for Batch AI
• Net Score = Sustainability Uplift - Distance Penalty
